The active ingredient

Meet HMB.

HMB is short for β-hydroxy β-methylbutyrate. Your body makes it from leucine, the amino acid in protein most tied to building muscle.

The catch: only about 5% of the leucine you eat becomes HMB. Studies on strength and recovery use around 3 grams a day, which is far more than food alone delivers.

So we put the full 3 grams in the can. You get it every time you crack one open.

Questions

Straight answers.

How much HMB is in Pepti?

3,000 mg (3 grams) in every 12.7 fl oz bottle or can. That's the daily amount most HMB research uses, so one Pepti covers it.

Does it actually taste like cola?

Yes. Pepti is a full cola first: dark, crisp and carbonated, with zero sugar. The HMB is there to work, not to be tasted.

Who is Pepti for?

Anyone who trains and wants to protect the muscle they build: lifters, athletes, and active adults who want more from their training as they get older. Pepti is for adults.

Should I drink more than one a day?

One a day is the serving. It already gives you the 3 grams studied in research, and more isn't shown to work better.

Is it safe to take HMB?

HMB has been studied for decades and is generally well tolerated at around 3 grams a day. If you're pregnant, nursing, taking medication or have a medical condition, check with your doctor first.
